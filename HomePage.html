/Gunite Pools

Design your custom pool experience.

Elite Pools also specializes in building and remodeling commercial and residential gunite pools (also called concrete pools or shotcrete pools).  A gunite pool is the one type of pool that allows the client and the builder to use their creativity and experience to design a pool that truly is custom.  The options in the design are only limited by your budget.

Gunite construction features can include:

Built in and Stand Alone Spill over Spas
Tanning ledges/ Sun shelves with Spill overs
Full Width Entry Steps and Beach Entries
Customized Benches and Seats
Customized Swim Lanes
Pebble Tech Speciality Aggregate Surfaces

About Our Company

A Quality Pool Company is a local, owner-operated business focused on doing pool and concrete work the right way – with experience, transparency, and respect for your property.

We specialize in pool inspections, full leak detection, openings and closings, liner replacements, equipment upgrades, and new pool installs. On top of that, we offer decorative concrete, pool decks, and other custom finishes to pull the entire backyard together.

Because we also run our own pumping and excavation equipment, we control the entire process from start to finish instead of juggling a bunch of random subcontractors. That means better quality, better communication, and fewer surprises.

Whether you need a thorough inspection before you buy or sell a home, help fixing an ongoing issue, or a complete pool and deck project, we're here to make sure the job gets done right and you know exactly what's going on every step of the way.
