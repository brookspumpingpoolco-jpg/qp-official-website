PoolFlowPro Features

Completely Customizable System

Run by pool business owners, built by pool professionals. If you need something, you call us-we build it.

Invoicing & Estimates

Create professional invoices and estimates with auto-numbering, templates, recurring invoices, and payment tracking.

Team Management

Manage your team, assign roles, track permissions, and control access to features and data.

Customer Management

Organize customer information, track interactions, manage contact details, and maintain customer history.

Inspections

Conduct full pool inspections, record findings, take photos, and generate detailed inspection reports.

Pool Calculator

Calculate pool volume, chemical needs, measurements, and material requirements for accurate estimates.

Quick Quote

Generate fast, accurate quotes for pool services with pre-configured pricing and item lists.

Item List / Price Book

Maintain comprehensive price books for materials, labor, and services with easy search and categorization.

Payment Processing

Accept payments via Stripe, track payment history, send payment links, and manage partial payments.

Project Management

Track projects from start to finish, manage milestones, link invoices, and monitor project progress.

Reporting & Analytics

Generate detailed reports, track revenue, analyze profit margins, and export data for accounting.

Photo Management

Capture and organize inspection photos, upload from gallery, and attach photos to jobs and invoices.

Job Scheduling

Schedule jobs, track status, sync with Square appointments, view routes, and manage daily schedules.

Need Something Custom?

PoolFlowPro is completely customizable. If you need a feature that's not listed here, just call us and we'll build it for you. We're run by pool business owners and built by pool professionals who understand your needs.
